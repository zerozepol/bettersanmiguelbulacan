## Remaining source gaps

- Complete current annual budget, appropriation ordinance, actual revenue and expenditure statements.
- Barangay hall addresses, public contacts and opening hours.
- Complete council roster including ex officio members and committee assignments.
- Additional service summaries, updated charter editions, and current fee confirmation.
- Remaining project detail pages, independent completion evidence and 2026 project coverage.
- Full legislative texts, subsequent amendments and complete historical archive.

Every listing includes its primary source, review date and any narrower caveat. Document links are separate from official index links. Do not interpret missing information as absence of a service, document or project. Do not total planning approvals as spending or use historic project figures as the current budget.
